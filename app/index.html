How it works

  1. 1

    Ask your AI to transcribe a score in LilyPond. Put the original score, the LilyPond PDF, and the LilyPond .ly file in the same folder and start a New Project.

  2. 2
    Proofread

    Compare two scores, such as a LilyPond rendering and its original source. Mark passages for correction.

  3. 3
    Send to AI

    Send the marked-up score to your AI with a pre-written prompt.

  4. 4
    Review Changes

    Load the AI’s corrected score and review the changes.
